# KidRota brand voice

**Like a friend who's done the school holidays a few times and has a system.**
Warm, a bit wry, practical. Never smug, never preachy about parenting.

## Do

- Write in British English (holidays, Mum, Nan, half-term, annual leave, colour).
- Lead with the parent's moment ("It's Sunday night and…"), not the feature.
- Be specific: "Thursday afternoon", "holiday club at £35 a day", "Grandad's
  golf day". Specific is relatable; generic is an ad.
- Keep captions short. One idea per post. First line must work on its own.
- One call to action per post, at the end.
- Credit the village: grandparents, friends and clubs are heroes, not problems.

## Don't

- Don't guilt anyone — about working, about clubs, about screen time.
- Don't assume a family shape. Say "the other parent" or "your co-parent", not
  "your husband". Not every family has two parents or grandparents nearby.
- Don't invent statistics, quotes, reviews or user counts. If a post needs a
  number, write `[STAT NEEDED: …]` and let Rob find a real, citable source.
- Don't claim features that don't exist (no iPhone app, no sync, no
  reminders-to-carers) — see the proof points in `campaign.md`.
- Don't name or tag specific schools, councils or children.
- Don't use stock "mum-boss" language, or "hacks".
- Don't post in parent groups unless the group rules allow it, and always add
  something useful before mentioning the app.

## Words we use

| Use | Instead of |
|---|---|
| plan, sorted, cover, gaps | optimise, solution, leverage |
| the other parent, co-parent | husband, wife, partner (unless the post is about a specific situation) |
| holiday club | childcare provider |
| free to plan, stays on your phone | secure cloud platform |

`#HolidayChildcare` `#ParentingUK` `#FamilyOrganisation` `#ChristmasHolidays`
`#SummerHolidays` `#EasterHolidays`

## Visual style

- Real phone screen recordings of the app over a plain background — the red
  gaps are the hero.
- App colours (see `src/styles/index.css`); no stock photos of smiling families.
- Text overlays big enough to read on a phone without sound.
