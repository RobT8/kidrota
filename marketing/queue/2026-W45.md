## 45-3 · Instagram + Facebook Page · Carousel · Holiday helpers
**Post on:** Saturday 9:30am
**Status:** [ ] approved  [ ] posted

**Hook:** Holiday clubs fill up fast at Christmas. 5 things to check before you book:

**Caption:**
Holiday clubs fill up fast at Christmas. 5 things to check before you book:

1. Which days are they actually open? Many close between Christmas and New Year.
2. Drop-off and pick-up times — do they fit your working day?
3. Can you use Tax-Free Childcare or childcare vouchers?
4. What do they need you to pack (lunch, wellies, a change of clothes)?
5. Will a friend be there? Booking the same days as another family makes
   swaps easier later.

Save this for when you're booking 📌

**Visual / shot list:**
1. Cover slide with the hook. One slide per tip. Last slide: "Save for later."

What worked for us was writing the whole fortnight out day by day first,
mornings and afternoons separately, and marking who's covering each. The gaps
jump out straight away and you can start asking people early instead of the
week before. Worth checking whether your holiday club is open between
Christmas and New Year too — lots aren't.
