KidRota

School holidays, sorted

Plan who's looking after the kids during every school break. See the gaps at a glance.

Your plans stay on your phone. No account, no sign-up, no servers of ours. Your plans are yours alone.

Get it on Google Play Free to download. KidRota Pro is an optional yearly subscription.

What it does

  • Add your children and the people who help — family, clubs, playdates, you
  • Plan each day with the times that suit you — morning, afternoon, all day or your own
  • Unplanned slots glow red, so gaps are impossible to miss
  • Plan one day, then copy it across the week in a tap
  • Works entirely offline
