# KidRota — Google Play store listing

Copy these into Play Console → Grow users → Store presence → Main store listing.
Keep this file in step with the app: the listing must not promise anything the app does not do.

```
KidRota School Holiday Planner
```

## Short description (80 max — this is 74)

```
Plan who has the kids every school holiday, and spot the gaps at a glance.
```

## Full description (4000 max — this is about 1,330)

```
Every school holiday, cover gets pieced together from grandparents, holiday clubs, playdates and annual leave. KidRota puts it all on one screen, so you can see at a glance who has the kids each day — and where the gaps are.

PLAN THE WHOLE BREAK
• Add a holiday and see every day laid out, weekdays only or the whole week
• Tap a morning or afternoon and pick who's covering it
• Need more detail? Switch to set times — Dad 8–10, Gran 10–3, Mum 3–6
• Copy a day across the week in one tap

SEE THE GAPS
• Anything not covered shows in red
• Home tells you how many gaps are left to fill and how long until the next break
• Optional reminders a few days before a holiday starts

SHARE IT
• Send the plan to your partner or a grandparent as a message they paste into their own KidRota
• Or share a picture of the week to any chat

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no ads
• Your plans stay on your phone — nothing is sent to us
• Export a backup file whenever you like

KIDROTA PRO
The free version plans one holiday for all your children. KidRota Pro is an optional yearly subscription that unlocks unlimited holidays and custom carer colours. It renews automatically each year until you cancel it in Google Play. If Pro ends, everything you've planned stays on your phone.
```
